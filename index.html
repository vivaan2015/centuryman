Step 3: Connect your GoDaddy domain to Cloudflare (The Most Important Step)

  1. In Cloudflare Pages, after deployment go to Custom domains > Set up a custom domain
  2. Type centuryman.in and also add www.centuryman.in

  4. Now go to GoDaddy > My Products > Domain > centuryman.in > Manage > Nameservers > Change > Custom > Paste those 2 Cloudflare nameservers > Save.
  5. Wait 30 min to 2 hours. Cloudflare will automatically give you FREE SSL (https). Your domain is now live.

If you don't want to change nameservers, you can also do it via DNS records: In GoDaddy DNS Management, add a CNAME record: Type: CNAME, Name: www, Value: your-project.pages.dev and for root, use A record to Cloudflare Pages IP. But changing nameservers is easier and better.
